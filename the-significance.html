— Amy Foster, The Gendered Anniversary: The Story of America's Women Astronauts, 2008
HISTORICAL ANALYSIS

The hard-fought battle over gender equality in the space program shows that the targeted dismantling of a problematic rule can be more effective than vague cultural shifts. Structural policy reform prevents institutional gatekeeping and is a focused target that is easier to achieve and can be a method of societal change.
